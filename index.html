About CMET

Policy-driven, implementation-focused research supporting India’s energy transition.

Centre for Mission on Energy Transition (CMET) is a policy-driven, implementation-focused research centre anchored at Maulana Azad National Institute of Technology, India, working to enable India’s transition towards a reliable, resilient, and low-carbon power system.

CMET has been established in collaboration with the New and Renewable Energy Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, to address emerging challenges in renewable energy integration, grid stability, and transmission planning.

CMET operates at the critical interface of government, utilities, industry and academia, translating advanced power system research into actionable solutions and on-ground implementation.

Our work spans the full spectrum—from high-impact technical assessment studies to field-level pilot demonstrations, ensuring that policy recommendations are not only theoretically sound but also practically viable.

Key Contributions

  • State-level Renewable Integration & Transmission Planning
  • Decentralized Reactive Power & Voltage Control Frameworks
  • Field Pilots & Demonstration Projects
  • Policy & Regulatory Engagement
  • Advanced Grid Analytics & Modeling

Vision & Mission

CMET’s purpose and impact-driven mission.

Vision Statement

To be a leading hub of innovation, research, and capacity building that accelerates the transition towards a sustainable, inclusive, and low-carbon energy future for India and the world.

Mission Statement

  • Conducting cutting-edge research on renewable energy integration, storage, and efficiency.
  • Developing and demonstrating innovative technologies for clean energy production and utilization.
  • Facilitating policy dialogue and stakeholder collaboration for equitable and sustainable energy systems.
  • Building capacity through education, training, and outreach to empower communities and industries.
  • Driving real-world impact through pilot projects, field demonstrations, and technology transfer.
