About CMET

A mission-driven centre for clean energy transformation

Established in collaboration with the Government of Madhya Pradesh, CMET works at the interface of government, utilities, industry, and academia.

Policy-led research Turning complex technical findings into actionable recommendations for implementation.
Applied innovation Linking advanced power system research with pilot projects and real-world deployment.
Institutional impact Supporting state-level planning, capacity building, and long-term energy resilience.
12+ active research and implementation programmes
4 major domains of focus across grid modernization
100% commitment to practical, measurable impact

Vision

To be a leading innovation hub for an inclusive, low-carbon energy future.

Accelerating a sustainable and resilient power system through research, training, and practical implementation.

Mission
  • Conduct cutting-edge research on renewable integration, storage, and efficiency.
  • Develop and demonstrate practical technologies for clean energy production and use.
  • Facilitate policy dialogue and stakeholder collaboration for equitable energy systems.
  • Build capacity through education, training, and outreach to communities and industries.
