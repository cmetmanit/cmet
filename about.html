WHO WE ARE

Bridging the Gap Between Technical Depth and Real-World Impact

The Centre for Mission on Energy Transition (CMET) is a mission-driven research institution working to enable India's transition towards a reliable, resilient, and low-carbon electrical power system.

Established in formal partnership with the New and Renewable Energy Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh, CMET addresses critical challenges in renewable energy integration, transmission network adequacy, and grid stability.

Our work spans the complete spectrumâ€”from rigorous power system modeling and hardware-in-the-loop validation to live feeder-level pilots under national schemes like PM-KUsUM. By grounding academic research in field realities, CMET ensures policy recommendations are not just theoretically sound, but practically viable and commercially scalable.

OUR GUIDING PRINCIPLEs

Vision & strategic Mission

A clear roadmap toward clean, reliable, and accessible power systems for India and the world.

OUR VIsION

To be a leading hub of innovation, research, and capacity building that accelerates the transition towards a sustainable, inclusive, and low-carbon energy future.

CMET strives to establish India as a global frontrunner in grid modernization, ensuring that renewable energy expansion is technically robust, socio-economically inclusive, and resilient against climate variability.

Target-Driven Impact Aligning with India's 500 GW renewable energy mission
Cutting-Edge Research Conducting foundational and applied studies on renewable energy integration, battery energy storage systems (BEss), and power system stability.
Innovative Technology Demonstration Developing, prototyping, and field-validating advanced solutions like solar inverter volt-VAR regulation, microgrid controllers, and demand-side management.
Policy Dialogue & stakeholder Collaboration Providing technical inputs to state electricity regulatory commissions, ministries, and DIsCOMs to inform forward-looking grid codes and tender frameworks.
Capacity Building & Outreach Empowering power utilities, academic scholars, and engineering professionals through hands-on technical workshops, simulation trainings, and knowledge-sharing.
Real-World Impact & Technology Transfer Driving verifiable results through on-ground pilot deployments, standard operating procedures (sOPs), and direct collaboration with industry partners.

OUR METHODOLOGY

How CMET Delivers Results

A proven 4-stage operational framework taking ideas from theoretical depth to institutional adoption.

01
system Insight & Modeling

Thorough network analysis, feeder data evaluation, and baseline calculations using ETAP, Pss®E, and load flow algorithms.

02
Hardware-in-the-Loop Testing

Real-time simulation on OPAL-RT 5600, microgrid emulators, and smart controllers to validate control strategies under stress conditions.

03
Field Pilot Deployment

Live on-ground implementation on distribution feeders and solar plants in collaboration with DIsCOMs and technology vendors.

04
Policy Integration & scaling

Formulation of standard Operating Procedures (sOPs), policy briefs, and regulatory frameworks for state-wide scaling.
