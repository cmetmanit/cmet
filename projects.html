Ongoing Projects

sustainable Rural Microgrid (CPRI)

Design and development of intelligent control strategies for rural microgrid systems with integrated renewable energy sources.

Key Highlights:
  • Planning-based controller for load prioritization
  • Co-optimization of distributed energy resources
  • Real-time load scheduling and shedding
  • Price-based operational strategy
  • Market-oriented microgrid design
Ongoing

PM-KUsUM Integration study (CEEW)

Assessment of cost-effective integration of PM-KUsUM solar plants into distribution networks.

Key Highlights:
  • Feeder-level analysis using DIsCOM data
  • Hosting capacity assessment
  • seasonal load variation modeling
  • solar penetration optimization
  • Grid constraint evaluation
Ongoing

solar Inverter Pilot Project (GIZ)

Implementation of inverter-based voltage regulation for improving distribution network performance.

Key Highlights:
  • 2 MW solar plant deployment (Ujjain)
  • PPC-based real-time control
  • Voltage support validation
  • Reactive power control using inverters
  • Field-tested implementation
Ongoing

Completed Projects

scaling up KUsUM-A & C Projects (RUMsL)

Technical analysis for optimal deployment of solar projects under KUsUM schemes.

Key Highlights:
  • Optimal injection point identification
  • Reduction in substation upgrade requirements
  • sOP for feeder capacity planning
  • Voltage and loss analysis
  • CUF-based performance improvement
Completed

Reactive Power Management in Renewable Grid

Utilization of solar inverters for reactive power support and grid stability enhancement.

Key Highlights:
  • Demonstrated on MANIT 230 kW solar plant
  • Inverter-based reactive power injection
  • Reduced dependence on capacitor banks
  • Improved voltage profile
  • Regulatory relevance
Completed

Technical Loss Estimation (MPMKVVCL)

Evaluation of technical losses in distribution systems to improve efficiency.

Key Highlights:
  • Loss analysis for 33 kV and 11 kV feeders
  • Identification of high-loss zones
  • simplified calculation methodology
  • Network optimization strategies
  • Practical utility recommendations
Completed

Transmission Adequacy & storage Optimization (WRI)

Key Highlights:

  • Grid reinforcement identification
  • storage placement optimization
  • system reliability enhancement
Completed
