Advisory Board

The Centre for Mission on Energy Transition (CMET) is envisioned as a high-impact institutional platform combining policy, modelling, and implementation expertise. The Advisory Board of CMET brings together leading experts from academia, industry, utilities, and regulatory institutions to ensure that CMET's work remains technically robust, policy-relevant, and aligned with emerging sectoral needs.
